# My Morning Routine

Wake up at 6:30 AM without snooze. Drink a glass of water immediately.

## The Flow

1. **Movement** (15 min) - Stretching and light exercise
2. **Mindfulness** (10 min) - Meditation or journaling
3. **Learning** (30 min) - Read or watch educational content
4. **Planning** (10 min) - Review calendar, set daily priorities

## What Works

- No phone for the first hour
- Preparing clothes the night before
- Same wake time on weekends

## What Doesn't Work

- Checking email before breakfast
- Skipping exercise when tired
- Complex breakfast preparations

The key insight: consistency beats intensity. A simple routine you follow daily outperforms an elaborate one you abandon after a week.
